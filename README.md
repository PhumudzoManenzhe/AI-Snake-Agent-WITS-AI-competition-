# AI-snake
This project is an intelligent Snake agent developed for the AI Snake competition hosted at the University of the Witwatersrand (Wits). The goal of this agent is to compete in a multi-snake environment where each snake must survive, grow, and strategically eliminate opponents. The agent is designed to make decisions in real time using artificial intelligence, pathfinding, and space control techniques. It adapts its behaviour dynamically based on the state of the game, the positions of opponents, and the level of risk present on the board.

The agent works by receiving structured input from the competition system every turn. This input includes the board dimensions, apple position, snake positions, snake lengths, obstacles, and the current game state. Using this information, the agent constructs a matrix-based representation of the game board. Each cell in the grid represents either empty space, a snake body, a snake head, or an obstacle. All snakes participating in the competition are drawn on the board with their respective identifiers. This allows the agent to simulate the environment and reason about safe and unsafe areas.

The primary strategy of the agent is based on the A* pathfinding algorithm. The snake uses A* to compute the fastest and safest path to the apple while avoiding collisions with other snakes. During this process, the agent considers both snake bodies and possible head-to-head collisions. It also evaluates risk by avoiding crowded areas and potential traps. If a safe path to the apple exists, the snake prioritises reaching the apple in order to grow efficiently and maintain a competitive advantage.

If the apple is unreachable, too risky, or no safe path exists, the agent switches to a fallback strategy known as Safe Mode. In this mode, the snake focuses on survival rather than growth. It selects moves that maximise the amount of free space available and avoids tight regions that could lead to being trapped. The agent also attempts to occupy central areas of the board. Staying near the middle increases the chances of reaching newly spawned apples faster and provides greater flexibility for movement. Even in Safe Mode, the snake continues to use pathfinding and spatial reasoning to avoid collisions and dangerous positions.

The agent also includes aggressive behaviour. When the snake detects a nearby opponent that is smaller in length, it may attempt to trap or block that opponent. This involves cutting off escape routes or blocking access to apples. This strategy helps reduce competition and increases the chances of long-term survival.

In addition to aggressive behaviour, the snake can enter a full Attack Mode when it dominates the game. This occurs when the snake is significantly longer than other competitors, specifically when it leads the board by at least 20 units or when its length exceeds 70 while still leading. In this mode, the agent targets the largest opponent and attempts to eliminate them by attacking their head or trapping them. This prevents strong opponents from surpassing the agent’s length. If another snake becomes stronger, the agent returns to focusing on apple collection instead of hunting.

Throughout the game, the agent continuously evaluates reachable space, connectivity of regions, and the risk of being trapped. It also predicts possible head collisions and avoids dangerous encounters. This ensures that the agent prioritises long-term survival over short-term gains. The overall strategy combines efficient growth, survival, space control, and competitive dominance.

This project demonstrates the use of artificial intelligence, heuristic search, and multi-agent strategy in a competitive environment. It was implemented in Java and focuses on real-time decision making, optimisation, and adaptive behaviour. Future improvements may include machine learning for opponent prediction, reinforcement learning, and adaptive heuristics to further enhance performance.

This agent was developed as part of the AI Snake competition at the University of the Witwatersrand and reflects a strong interest in artificial intelligence, competitive programming, and algorithm design.
